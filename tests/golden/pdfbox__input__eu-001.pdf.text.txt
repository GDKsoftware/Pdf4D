| E-PRTR pollutants and their thresholds\x20
| \x20
| A facility has to report data under E-PRTR if it fulfils the following criteria: \x20
| • the facility falls under at least one of the 65 E-PRTR economic activities. The\x20
| activities are also reported using a statistical classification of economic activities\x20
| (NACE rev 2) \x20
| • the facility has a capacity exceeding at least one of the E-PRTR capacity\x20
| thresholds \x20
| • the facility releases pollutants or transfers waste off-site which exceed specific\x20
| thresholds set out in Article 5 of the E-PRTR Regulation. These thresholds for\x20
| releases of pollutants are specified for each media - air, water and land - in Annex\x20
| II of the E-PRTR Regulation.\x20
