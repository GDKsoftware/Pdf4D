| A forbearance is a period during which you are allowed\x20
| to postpone making payments temporarily, allowed an\x20
| extension of time for making payments, or temporarily\x20
| allowed to make smaller payments than scheduled. A\x20
| forbearance can be a mandatory forbearance, meaning that\x20

| for the forbearance and supply all supporting\x20
| documentation. A forbearance can also be a discretionary\x20

| forbearance, but is not required to do so.\x20
| The holder of your Direct Loans is the Department. The\x20

| to your loans. References to “your loan holder” on this form\x20
| mean either your loan holder or your servicer.\x20
| A subsidized loan is a Direct Subsidized Loan, a Direct\x20
| Subsidized Consolidation Loan, a Federal Subsidized\x20
| Stafford Loan, portions of some Federal Consolidation\x20
| Loans, Federal Perkins Loans, NDSL, and Defense Loans.\x20
| An unsubsidized loan is a Direct Unsubsidized Loan, a\x20
| Direct Unsubsidized Consolidation Loan, a Direct PLUS\x20
| Loan, a Federal Unsubsidized Stafford Loan, a Federal PLUS\x20
| Loan, a Federal SLS, and portions of some Federal\x20
| Consolidation Loans.\x20

| SECTION 5: DEFINITIONS
| A forbearance is a period during which you are allowed\x20
| to postpone making payments temporarily, allowed an\x20
| extension of time for making payments, or temporarily\x20
| allowed to make smaller payments than scheduled. A\x20
| forbearance can be a mandatory forbearance, meaning that\x20
| your loan holder must grant the forbearance if you qualify\x20
| for the forbearance and supply all supporting\x20
| documentation. A forbearance can also be a discretionary\x20
| forbearance, meaning that your loan holder may grant the\x20
| forbearance, but is not required to do so.\x20
| The holder of your Direct Loans is the Department. The\x20
| holder of your FFEL Program loans may be a lender,\x20
| guaranty agency, secondary market, or the Department. The\x20
| holder of your Perkins Loans is an institution of higher\x20
| education or the Department. Your loan holder may use a\x20
| servicer to handle billing and other communications related\x20
| to your loans. References to “your loan holder” on this form\x20
| mean either your loan holder or your servicer.\x20
| A subsidized loan is a Direct Subsidized Loan, a Direct\x20
| Subsidized Consolidation Loan, a Federal Subsidized\x20
| Stafford Loan, portions of some Federal Consolidation\x20
| Loans, Federal Perkins Loans, NDSL, and Defense Loans.\x20
| An unsubsidized loan is a Direct Unsubsidized Loan, a\x20
| Direct Unsubsidized Consolidation Loan, a Direct PLUS\x20
| Loan, a Federal Unsubsidized Stafford Loan, a Federal PLUS\x20
| Loan, a Federal SLS, and portions of some Federal\x20
| Consolidation Loans.\x20
| The William D. Ford Federal Direct Loan (Direct Loan)\x20
| Program includes Federal Direct Stafford/Ford (Direct\x20
| Subsidized) Loans, Federal Direct Unsubsidized Stafford/
| Ford (Direct Unsubsidized) Loans, Federal Direct PLUS\x20
| (Direct PLUS) Loans, and Federal Direct Consolidation\x20
| (Direct Consolidation) Loans.
| Capitalization is the addition of unpaid interest to the\x20
| principal balance of your loan. Capitalization causes more\x20
| interest to accrue over the life of your loan and may cause\x20
| your monthly payment amount to increase. Interest never\x20
| capitalizes on Perkins Loans. Table 1 (below) provides an\x20
| example of the monthly payments and the total amount\x20
| repaid for a $30,000 unsubsidized loan. The example loan\x20
| has a 6% interest rate and the example deferment or\x20
| forbearance lasts for 12 months and begins when the loan\x20
| entered repayment. The example compares the effects of\x20
| paying the interest as it accrues or allowing it to be\x20
| capitalized.\x20
| A co-maker is one of the two individuals who are joint\x20
| borrowers on a Direct or Federal Consolidation Loan or a\x20
| Federal PLUS Loan. Both co-makers are equally responsible\x20
| for repaying the full amount of the loan.\x20
| A deferment is a period during which you are entitled\x20
| to postpone repayment of your loans. Interest is not\x20
| generally charged to you during a deferment on your\x20
| subsidized loans. Interest is always charged to you during a\x20
| deferment on your unsubsidized loans. On loans made\x20
| under the Perkins Loan Program, all deferments are\x20
| followed by a post-deferment grace period of 6 months,\x20
| during which time you are not required to make payments.\x20
| An endorser is an individual who signs a promissory\x20
| note and agrees to pay the loan if the borrower does not.\x20
| The Federal Family Education Loan (FFEL) Program\x20
| includes Federal Stafford Loans, Federal PLUS Loans, Federal\x20
| Consolidation Loans, and Federal Supplemental Loans for\x20
| Students (SLS).\x20
| The Federal Perkins Loan (Perkins Loan) Program\x20
| includes Federal Perkins Loans, National Direct Student\x20
| Loans (NDSL), and National Defense Student Loans (Defense\x20
| Loans).
