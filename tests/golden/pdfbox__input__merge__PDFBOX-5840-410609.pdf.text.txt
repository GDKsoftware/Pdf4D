| Chan TTC, Sadovy Y (1998) Profile of the marine aquarium fish trade in Hong Kong. \x20
| Aquarium Sciences and Conservation. 2(4):197-213
| A market survey and review of government statistics were carried out to establish imports and\x20
| exports of marine ornamental fishes into and out of Hong Kong, and to examine the local trade\x20
| in terms of volume, value and species composition. Official government import figures for\x20
| marine aquarium fishes were available from 1984 to 1991 and from 1997 and 1998. When\x20
| compared with net imports, export and re export volumes over the 15 year period were\x20
| small, indicating that most imports entered the local market, or were exported unrecorded.\x20
| The market survey of marine aquarium shops in Hong Kong was carried out between\x20
| August 1996 and January 1997. From this survey, an annual estimate of 957,563 coral reef\x20
| fish was calculated for the local trade, valued at HK$57,453,780, with a mean retail price of\x20
| HK$60 per fish. These figures account for an estimated 2 3% of the global value and\x20
| volume of marine aquarium fish trade, according to 1992 figures and, compared with\x20
| government figures, indicate that official declarations of imports are underreported by at\x20
| least 2 3 fold. A total of 342 marine aquarium fish species, from 49 families, were recorded\x20
| http://www.nal.usda.gov/awic/pubs/Fishwelfare/aquar.htm (4 of 6)9/16/2003 9:05:30 AM

| with about 60% belonging to the families Labridae, Chaetodontidae, Pomacanthidae and\x20
| Pomacentridae. It was estimated that a large proportion of the trade was in juvenile fishes\x20
| and almost all fish were less than 10 cm in standard length. It was common to see fishes in\x20
| poor condition, species hard to maintain in captivity, or those listed for conservation\x20
| concern, on sale. There are no regulations for local traders of live marine fishes other than\x20
| those relating to either protected species or animal welfare. Licensing of traders in marine\x20
| fishes could be introduced under existing Hong Kong legislation if fishes were to be re\x20
| classified as animals. This is strongly recommended as a step towards regulating and\x20
