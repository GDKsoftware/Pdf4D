# page <n> [<platform>] # <reason>
page 1 # 150 DPI only (72 DPI equals): The tiling cells are drawn on images of their own as well, which adds to the groups. Not fixed, ruled a deliberate deviation (docs/porting.md): PDFBox draws the content of a transparency group on an image in a y-up space that only has the scale (PageDrawer.TransparencyGroup: xform = getScaleInstance) and flips it onto the page with a whole-pixel offset, so the pixel grid of the group is shifted against the grid of the page by the fraction of the page height in pixels (400 pt at 150 DPI is 833.33 px). At 72 DPI the height is whole and the grids agree. Pdf4D rasterizes the group on the grid of the page. The pages of the same size without a group (colors-device, colors-special, clip) equal PDFBox at 150 DPI since the stroke normalization and the clip follow Java2D.
